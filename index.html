Questions brands ask us

Do you buy inventory or work on consignment?

We buy wholesale with standard purchase orders. You get paid for the goods, not for what we sell later.

Will you respect our pricing?

Yes. We follow your MAP and pricing policy, and we put that in writing.

What about the unauthorized sellers already on our listing?

Once we are authorized, we work with you through Brand Registry to address them.

Do we keep control of our brand?

Always. You own the brand and approve listing content.

What is the minimum order?

We work with your MOQ. Tell us your terms on the first call.
