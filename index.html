For Brand OwnersWhy Brands Work With Us

When a brand has no authorized partner on Amazon, anyone can sell its products. Here is what changes when we become yours.

Price undercuttingUnauthorized sellers race to the bottom.

We follow your MAP policy in writing and work with you through Brand Registry to clean up the listing.

Weak listingsWrong titles, poor images, missing details.

We rebuild titles, images, bullets and A+ content, and you approve every change.

StockoutsLost Buy Box and lost ranking.

We forecast demand and reorder on time to keep inventory in stock.

No visibilityYou do not know who sells what, or at what price.

You get a monthly report on units sold, selling price and inventory.

Questions brands ask us

Do you buy inventory or work on consignment?

We buy wholesale with standard purchase orders. You get paid for the goods, not for what we sell later.

Will you respect our pricing?

Yes. We follow your MAP and pricing policy, and we put that in writing.

Where do you sell?

On Amazon U.S. through our own seller account. Other channels only if you approve them. We share our storefront and account details on the first call.

Where is your inventory held?

At our fulfillment hubs in Chicago, IL and Clifton, NJ, and in Amazon fulfillment centers.

Do we keep control of our brand?

Always. You own the brand and approve listing content.

What is the minimum order?

We work with your MOQ. Tell us your terms on the first call.
