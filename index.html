Questions brands ask us

How do we know you are a real company?

WORLDWIDEDEALER LLC is registered in Delaware, File No. 5820021, with an office in Woodside, New York. Our founder deals with you directly, and we will share our company documents before you sign anything.

Do you charge fees?

No. There are no setup fees or commissions. We earn our margin on the stock we buy from you.

Are you a 3PL or a fulfillment service?

No. We buy your products outright. You get a purchase order, and the stock is ours to sell under the terms we agreed.

Will you undercut my pricing?

We sell inside the pricing policy you give us. It goes into our written terms before the first order.

Where will my products be sold?

On Amazon and the other online channels we agree with you, in the territories we agree. All of it is set out in writing. If you already have an exclusive partner somewhere, we stay out of that territory.

What do you need from us to start?

Your price list, your minimum order and your pricing policy. After that we agree terms and you authorize us in writing.

Ready to talk about your brand?

Tell us what you sell and where you want to grow. You will hear from our founder directly.
