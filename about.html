About WORLDWIDEDEALER

An authorized distributor for consumer brands, based in New York.

Who we are

WORLDWIDEDEALER LLC is a Delaware company, formed in 2021, with its office in Woodside, New York. We have worked in e-commerce since 2019.

We buy from brands at wholesale, hold the stock and sell it as an authorized partner on Amazon and other online channels. A brand gets a paying customer and a partner who follows its rules.

Serhat Topal

Founder and Head of Purchasing. Brands deal directly with Serhat on terms, orders and reorders, so there is always one person who knows your account.
